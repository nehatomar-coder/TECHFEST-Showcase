01. About Techfest

Techfest is the annual techno-management festival of the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay. Recognized globally and supported by prestigious international and national organizations like UNESCO and UNICEF, it serves as the ultimate crucible for groundbreaking innovations, intense technical competitions, global tech summits, and immersive workshops.
